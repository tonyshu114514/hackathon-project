Find It: Site Screener

Housing-element sites, ranked two ways, with HCD permit, opportunity, pollution and contamination checks.

Prototype for screening only. Not legal, zoning or real estate advice. Rankings use ASSUMPTION weights; confirm every site with the city and a professional.

Top 5 to check first

Open land, lower-income capacity, size, transit and high-opportunity area, minus hazard, contamination and pollution penalties. Sites with a recent building, or permitted or entitled since 2021 per HCD, are left out.

    Top 5 likely stale

    Listed sites that may already be used: a building dated at or after the cutoff year, units the city reported to HCD as permitted, completed or entitled, then high footprint coverage.

            What this does not check
            • Ownership and willingness to sell. Parcel data has no owner field here.
            • Which state law (SB 9, SB 684, AB 2011, SB 4) applies. Hazard flags are screening signals, not each law's legal tests.
            • Contamination beyond DTSC EnviroStor (State Water Board GeoTracker leak sites are not loaded), and any Phase I review.
            • Water and sewer capacity, and city objective standards.
            • Permits the city has not yet reported to HCD; APR data lags by up to a year, and matching is by APN only.
