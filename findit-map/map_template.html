Find It: Site Screener

Housing-element sites, ranked two ways, with HCD permit, opportunity, pollution and contamination checks.

Prototype for screening only. Not legal, zoning or real estate advice. Rankings use ASSUMPTION weights; confirm every site with the city and a professional.

            • Water and sewer capacity, and city objective standards.
